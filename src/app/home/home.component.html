About Us

Central India’s leading
waterpark, adventure,
amusement park and resort

Welcome to Waghville Waterpark & Resort, where the perfect blend of adventure, luxury, and natural beauty awaits you. Dive into excitement with thrilling slides, relaxing lazy rivers, and refreshing wave pools. Nestled in the heart of a sprawling 54-acre oasis near the enchanting Bor Tiger forest, our resort is a haven for thrill-seekers, nature lovers, and those seeking a blissful escape from the routine. Perfect for families and thrill-seekers alike, Waghville promises endless aquatic adventures. Join us for a splashing good time!
