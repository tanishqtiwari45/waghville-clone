Our Rules

Your safety and enjoyment are our top priorities at WaghVille. To ensure a pleasant experience for all guests, we kindly ask that you familiarize yourself with our park rules. By adhering to these guidelines, we can maintain a safe and enjoyable environment for everyone. Thank you for your cooperation.

  • 1. For corporate events or party, before 5 day menu should be decided.
  • 2. For large group and party, we have customized food option also available.
  • 3. For any special requirement, prior booking must.
  • 4. For waterpark and Adventures, Below 33 inch Free Entry, 33 inch to 42 inch Half Ticket (400 Including Costume), and above 42 inch Full Ticket (700 Including Costume) Applicable.
  • 5. Food Timing – Customer who took Stay Package or Day Food Package:
    Breakfast: 10 AM - 11 AM
    Lunch: 1.30 PM - 3 PM
    High Tea: 4 PM - 5.30 PM
    Dinner: 9 PM - 10.30 PM
  • 6. We have facility of Customized cooking of NON veg (Fish, Gavrani, Mutton) as extra 600/KG charges.
  • 7. 2 Complementary Water Bottle For Room Guest + Toothpaste + Soap + Oil Pouch.
  • 8. Costume and Locker rent as applicable Extra (Rs.100 and Rs.80 Respectively).
  • 9. Waterpark Timing 10 AM To 4 PM and adventures Timing 4 PM to 6 PM.
  • 10. Refundable Security deposit 200/person for Costume and 100 for Locker applicable at the time of Entry.
  • 11. Bulk Booking and customize offer also available with discounted price.
  • 12. For Birthday/Anniversary or any special day celebration kindly order before the programme.
  • 13. We have special offer for Member, Corporates and our Associates.
  • 14. School PICNIC/TRIP offer applicable only above 60+ Students.
  • 15. Menu can be customized only for Groups 10 and Above, as per prior Booking before 5 Days.
  • 16. Non Veg, (Chicken) can be serve as per Availability.
  • 17. Menu can be customized only for Groups 10 and Above, as per prior Booking before 5 Days.
  • 18. Starter not Including on The food Package (Stay and Day Package). Charges as per Available Rates.
  • 19. During rush hour, buffet arrangement will provide. Rs. 600/KG charges for customized chicken and mutton cooking. Valid Food coupon must carry as per package.

Waghville Waterpark and Resorts is a must-visit destination for anyone looking for a fun-filled day out or a relaxing getaway in Nagpur. With its exciting water rides, great food, and comfortable accommodation options, it is the perfect place to enjoy some quality time with family and friends.
