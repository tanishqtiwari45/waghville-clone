15 Years Legacy

Waghville Waterpark & Resort stands as a testament to the founders' initial vision and commitment, offering a multifaceted destination where entertainment, relaxation, and community development harmoniously coexist.

🌿

Roots in Agro Tourism

Waghville has a humble beginning rooted in agro tourism and farmhouses, taking its first steps in 2009. The founders, Narendra Wagh and Surendra Wagh, envisioned a unique destination that not only celebrated the natural beauty of Umari, Wagh but also provided a haven for entertainment and relaxation. This initial foray laid the foundation for what would later become Waghville Waterpark & Resort.

👥

Mission to Serve the Community

The driving force behind Waghville's establishment was not only to create a recreational retreat but also to contribute to the local community. The founders aimed to provide employment opportunities for the people of Umari, Wagh, thereby fostering economic growth and sustainability in the region.

Waghville Waterpark and Resorts is a must-visit destination for anyone looking for a fun-filled day out or a relaxing getaway in Nagpur. With its exciting water rides, great food, and comfortable accommodation options, it is the perfect place to enjoy some quality time with family and friends.
